to donate.

Whatever you like, whenever you like. It all goes into keeping the mod alive: hours of work, testing and whatever it takes to keep it running.

Then write to us on Discord with the name you want to appear under, a quote if you like and your Steam profile, and we add you to the roll. It shows how much you gave; if you would rather it did not, just tell us.

It's all cosmetic.

Donating never gives you an edge in a match: online play is just as fair for everyone.
